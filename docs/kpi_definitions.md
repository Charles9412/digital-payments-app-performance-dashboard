# KPI Definitions

This document provides business-friendly interpretations of the dashboard KPIs. Definitions are intentionally generic for portfolio use.

## App Operations
The total volume of payment app transactions or operations completed within the selected period.

## Digital Adoption Rate
The share of eligible activity or users adopting the digital app channel relative to total potential usage.

## Estimated Hours Saved
An estimated measure of operational time reduced through app usage versus traditional/manual alternatives.

## Total Registered Users
The total number of users registered in the app as of the selected period.

## New Registered Users
The number of users who newly registered during the selected period.

## Average Monthly Users
The average number of users active per month across the selected time range.

## Unique Active Users
The count of distinct users who performed at least one app activity during the selected period.

## Monthly App Contracts
The number of contracts initiated or completed through the app each month.

## Unique App Users
The count of distinct users who used the app, typically reported for comparison with total registrations.

## Lower Adoption States
States or regions showing comparatively lower adoption levels based on the portfolio dataset.
